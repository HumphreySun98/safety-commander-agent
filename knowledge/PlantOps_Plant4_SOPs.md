# ACME Precision — Plant 4 Standard Operating Procedures (site-specific knowledge)

Internal reference. Maps the OSHA standards to how Plant 4 actually runs. Use alongside
the site safety policy.

## Layout & zones
- Zone W: designated welding/hot-work bay (screened); sparks expected there only.
- Green walkway: painted pedestrian-protected lane down the east wall; PPE-optional.
- Forklift aisle: the central yellow-bordered lane; full PPE; shared with pedestrians.
- Press cells: power-press machine cells along the north wall.

## SOP-FK-01 — Forklift material moves (per OSHA 1910.178)
- Loads must be stable and within rated capacity. Tiered stillage bins must be restrained
  and must NOT be stacked so high they obscure the operator's forward view — if the view
  is blocked, travel in reverse (load trailing).
- Keep ≥ 3 m from pedestrians on foot; sound the horn at the press-cell cross aisle.
- The specific maximum stack height / bin count for this site is set in the editable
  safety policy and may change — follow the current policy value.

## SOP-PR-02 — Power press operation (per OSHA 1910.212 / 1910.217)
- Guards must be in place while the press can cycle. Never reach into the die area on a
  live press. Clearing jams or servicing requires lockout/tagout (SOP-LT-04).
- Eye protection mandatory at all press cells.

## SOP-LT-04 — Lockout/Tagout
- Any "intervention" on a machine (reaching in, clearing, adjusting) requires the press to
  be locked out and verified at zero energy first. An unauthorized intervention on a live
  machine is a serious, reportable hazard.

## SOP-HK-06 — Housekeeping & spills
- Liquid spills on walking/working surfaces are contained and signed immediately; for
  chemical spills, isolate the area and use the matching SDS spill procedure.

## Incident escalation
- Near-miss: log + notify shift supervisor + EHS. Life-safety (fire/smoke outside Zone W,
  person under a load, chemical release): emergency escalation + clear the area.
