# OSHA — Lockout/Tagout (1910.147), Fire & Hot Work (1910.157 / 1910.106 / 1910.252)

Source: U.S. OSHA General Industry standards (public domain). Site reference library.

## Lockout/Tagout — 1910.147
- Before servicing or maintenance where unexpected energization or start-up could occur,
  energy sources shall be isolated and locked/tagged out (1910.147(c)(1)).
- Each authorized employee applies their own lock; the machine is verified at zero energy
  before work begins. Removing a guard for "authorized intervention" without LOTO is a
  violation.

## Portable fire extinguishers — 1910.157
- Extinguishers shall be mounted, located, and identified so they are readily accessible
  (1910.157(c)(1)) and the path to them kept clear. Travel distance to an extinguisher
  for Class A hazards shall not exceed 75 ft.

## Flammable liquids — 1910.106
- Flammable and combustible liquids shall be stored and handled to control ignition
  sources. Open flame, smoking, and spark-producing work are restricted near flammable
  storage and dispensing.

## Hot work / welding & cutting — 1910.252
- 1910.252(a): Hot work (welding, cutting, grinding) requires fire prevention precautions:
  a permit, a fire watch, removal/shielding of combustibles within 35 ft, and only in
  approved/designated areas. Open flame or visible smoke outside a designated, permitted
  hot-work area is an emergency.

## Exits & egress — 1910.37
- Exit routes shall be kept free of obstructions. Fire exits, alarm pulls, and electrical
  panels must remain unobstructed (typically ≥ 36 in clearance in front of panels).
