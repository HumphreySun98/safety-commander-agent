# OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178 — Powered Industrial Trucks (forklifts)

Source: U.S. OSHA General Industry standard (public domain). Site reference library.

## Load handling and capacity
- 1910.178(o)(2): Only stable and safely arranged loads within the rated capacity of
  the truck shall be handled. The load must not exceed the truck's rated capacity shown
  on the data plate.
- 1910.178(o)(1): Caution shall be exercised when handling off-center, long, high, or
  tiered loads that may tip or fall. Tall/tiered loads must be stabilized or restrained.
- 1910.178(n)(7)(i)–(iii): A load engaging means shall be carried low; the load shall be
  tilted back where possible to improve stability.

## Obstructed view
- 1910.178(n)(6): If the load being carried obstructs the operator's forward view, the
  operator shall travel with the load trailing (in reverse). Traveling forward with a
  view-blocking load is prohibited.

## Pedestrians and travel
- 1910.178(m)(2): Pedestrians shall be given the right of way; maintain a safe distance
  (commonly ≥ 3 m / 10 ft) from a moving truck.
- 1910.178(n)(4): The driver shall slow down and sound the horn at cross aisles and other
  locations where vision is obstructed.
- 1910.178(m)(1): No person shall stand or pass under the elevated portion of any truck,
  whether loaded or empty.

## Unattended trucks
- 1910.178(m)(5)(i): A truck is "unattended" when the operator is 25 ft or more away, or
  leaves it in view but it is not in sight. Fully lower the forks, neutralize controls,
  shut off power, and set brakes.

## Operator training
- 1910.178(l): Only trained and evaluated operators may operate a powered industrial truck.
