# OSHA 29 CFR 1910 Subpart I — Personal Protective Equipment

Source: U.S. OSHA General Industry standards (public domain). Site reference library.

## General requirement — 1910.132
PPE shall be provided, used, and maintained wherever hazards of processes or environment
are present. The employer assesses the workplace (a hazard assessment) to determine what
PPE is required, and ensures employees use it.

## Eye and face protection — 1910.133
Eye/face protection is required where there is a reasonable probability of injury from
flying particles, molten metal, chemical splash, acids, or injurious light (welding).
Side protection is required where there is a hazard from flying objects. Required around
grinding, machining, welding, and chemical handling.

## Head protection — 1910.135
Protective helmets (hard hats) shall be worn where there is a potential for head injury
from falling or flying objects, or contact with electrical conductors. Required in areas
with overhead loads, cranes, or material handling.

## Hand protection — 1910.138
Appropriate hand protection is required when hands are exposed to cuts, abrasions,
punctures, chemical burns, or harmful temperature extremes (e.g. cut-resistant gloves for
sheet metal / blades).

## Hi-visibility note
High-visibility apparel is required (per site policy and ANSI/ISEA 107) wherever workers
on foot are exposed to powered industrial trucks or vehicle traffic, so operators can see
them in aisles and at intersections.
