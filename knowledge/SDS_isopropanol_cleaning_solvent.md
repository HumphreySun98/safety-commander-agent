# Safety Data Sheet (excerpt) — Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) cleaning solvent

Site reference for spill scenarios. Excerpted SDS (illustrative).

## Section 2 — Hazards
- Highly flammable liquid and vapour (H225). Causes serious eye irritation (H319). May
  cause drowsiness or dizziness (H336). Keep away from heat, sparks, open flame, hot
  surfaces. No smoking.

## Section 6 — Accidental release / spill response
- Eliminate all ignition sources. Ventilate the area.
- Stop the leak if safe. Contain and absorb the spill with non-combustible absorbent
  (sand, vermiculite); do NOT use combustible materials.
- Wear eye protection and chemical-resistant gloves. Avoid breathing vapour.
- A spill on a walking surface is also a slip hazard — sign and barrier the area.
- For large releases, isolate the area and escalate to trained spill response / EHS.

## Section 8 — Exposure controls / PPE
- Safety glasses or goggles; chemical-resistant gloves; adequate ventilation.

## Section 10 — Stability/reactivity
- Vapours may form explosive mixtures with air. Static discharge can ignite vapour during
  transfer; bond and ground containers.
