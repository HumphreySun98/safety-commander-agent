# OSHA 29 CFR 1910.212 — General Requirements for All Machines (machine guarding)

Source: U.S. OSHA General Industry standard (public domain). Site reference library.

## Guarding required
- 1910.212(a)(1): One or more methods of machine guarding shall protect operators and
  others from hazards such as those created by point of operation, ingoing nip points,
  rotating parts, flying chips, and sparks.
- 1910.212(a)(3)(ii): The point of operation of machines whose operation exposes an
  employee to injury shall be guarded. A missing, removed, or open guard on running
  equipment is a serious hazard.

## Reaching into machinery
- A worker reaching into the point of operation of running/energized equipment, or
  bypassing a guard, is exposed to amputation and crush hazards. If maintenance or
  clearing requires access, the machine must first be de-energized under lockout/tagout
  (see 1910.147). Authorized intervention requires LOTO or an equivalent safe procedure.

## Power presses (Subpart O reference)
- 1910.217 governs mechanical power presses: point-of-operation guarding, two-hand
  controls, and brake monitoring. Operators must never place hands in the die area while
  the press is capable of cycling.

## Anchoring
- 1910.212(b): Machines designed for a fixed location shall be securely anchored to
  prevent walking or moving.
