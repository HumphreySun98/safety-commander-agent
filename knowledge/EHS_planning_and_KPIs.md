# Industrial EHS — KPI standards & inspection/training cadence (planning reference)

Reference for the WEEK/MONTH planner so plans match real manufacturing EHS practice
(OSHA + industry norms). Public-domain / industry-standard guidance, paraphrased.

## Safety KPIs (for the MONTH roll-up)
Use a balanced scorecard: 2–3 lagging + 3–4 leading indicators.
- **Lagging (outcomes):** TRIR = recordable injuries × 200,000 / hours-worked; DART
  (days away/restricted/transferred); SIF (serious injury & fatality) rate.
- **Leading (preventive):** near-miss reporting frequency, training-completion %,
  on-time corrective-action closure %, inspections-completed %.
- **Industry targets:** training completion ≥ 90%; on-time corrective-action closure
  ≥ 85%; medium-risk corrective actions resolved within ~30 days.
- **Near-miss is a LEADING indicator:** a rising near-miss count usually means better
  reporting culture; strong near-miss programs see ~30–50% fewer recordable incidents.

## Inspection cadence (for the WEEK plan)
- **Forklifts / powered industrial trucks:** inspected DAILY before being placed in
  service (after EACH shift if run 24/7); documented pre-shift checklist (OSHA 1910.178).
- **Weekly:** scheduled walk-throughs of machine guarding, fire extinguishers, PPE
  compliance, housekeeping / aisle clearance, pedestrian–forklift separation.
- **Monthly:** emergency equipment checks, deeper compliance audits.

## Training cadence (for the WEEK plan)
- **Forklift operators:** initial training + evaluation; RE-EVALUATE at least every
  3 years; REFRESHER whenever a deficiency, near-miss, or incident occurs (1910.178(l)).
- **Toolbox talks:** short (5–15 min), held WEEKLY, topic tied to the week's actual
  hazards / near-misses.
- **Targeted training** immediately after a spike in a specific hazard (data-driven).

## Planning principle
Schedule highest-frequency / highest-severity hazards first; tie every item to the data
that triggered it and the policy clause / OSHA standard; name an owner. The agent
proposes; the safety committee confirms.
